GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Does AURELIA need internet during a service?

Bundled assets and local media are designed to work offline. Online assets need internet while previewing or using them until downloaded.

Can I use two monitors or an HDMI projector?

The app is designed for an extended-display workflow. Connect your projector, choose Extend displays in Windows, then select the audience output in AURELIA. Test with your actual projector before a live service.

Can I import my own videos and GIFs?

The Pro edition includes expanded local media tools. Some source formats may need conversion and DRM-protected or damaged files may not play.

Can I buy Pro right now?

Not through this starter website yet. The PayMongo checkout and post-payment delivery must be configured and tested before accepting real payments.
